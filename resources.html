Streaming Guides

These are basic tips to help support Red Velvet on major platforms:

  • On Spotify, avoid looping a single song—use a playlist of mixed tracks.
  • Like and save their albums to your library.
  • On YouTube, watch from official sources and avoid muted looping.
  • Interact with videos: like, comment, and share.
